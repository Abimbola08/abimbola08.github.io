Airports are among the most demanding road networks to operate: peaked demand, heavy curbside activity, and little room to expand. For MTI Project 2120 this work built and calibrated **VISSIM microsimulation models** of the Charlotte Douglas International Airport road network and evaluated alternatives including continuous flow intersections (CFI), restricted crossing U-turns (RCUT), mini roundabouts, and new access routes, measuring their effect on travel time reliability.
