As a Traffic Signal & Operations Engineer on the GDOT SigOps program at Gresham Smith, I configured, monitored, and retimed signal controllers using Q-Free MaxTime and MaxView, extracted and interpreted **Automated Traffic Signal Performance Measures (ATSPM)** to guide timing strategies, and built Power BI dashboards for ongoing performance monitoring. Timing alternatives were tested in VISSIM and TransModeler before deployment, and automation scripts cut the manual effort of routine data pulls substantially.
