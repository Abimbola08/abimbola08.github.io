Severe and fatal crashes are rare, so models trained on raw crash data tend to predict "no injury" almost every time. This project designed **synthetic data generation techniques tailored to nominal (categorical) crash predictors**, extending SMOTE-N and ADASYN-N, and benchmarked random forest and XGBoost severity models with and without treatment.

Under a Mineta Transportation Institute project (MTI 2320) the comparison was widened to statistical, machine learning, and deep learning models to establish which family performs best under differing data conditions. Results were published in *Transportation Research Record* and in the MTI final report.
