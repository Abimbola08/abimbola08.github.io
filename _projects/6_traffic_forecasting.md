On the GDOT Traffic Forecasting program I review traffic forecasting reports on behalf of the Office of Planning and Budgeting, conduct traffic impact analyses for proposed developments, and perform roundabout, intersection, and interchange analysis in SIDRA. Crash, volume, and roadway data are combined to identify high-risk locations and prioritize safety countermeasures, with spatial crash trends presented through GIS and Tableau.
