Public acceptance is one of the biggest non-technical barriers to autonomous vehicle deployment. Using survey data collected around a widely reported fatal AV crash, this work estimated **fixed- and random-parameter ordered logit models** of how pedestrians' and cyclists' safety perception of AVs varies with opinion change stemming from the crash.

Related collaborative work examined autonomous taxis, ride-sharing vehicles, and autonomous shuttles from a social-construct perspective, and identified the connected and automated vehicle data needs of North Carolina public agencies to build a data readiness framework for NCDOT.
