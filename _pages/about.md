Dr. Abimbola (Abim) Ogungbire is a Traffic/ITS Engineer with domain expertise in transportation operations and planning. He holds a Ph.D. in Transportation Systems from the William States Lee College of Engineering at the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://www.charlotte.edu/).

For his doctoral research, he worked on the development and validation of machine learning systems for weather-related crash prediction. More specifically, he demonstrated the innovative integration of geospatial analytics and advanced machine learning for weather-related crash risk mapping, severity prediction, and forecasting. Alongside his doctoral research, Abim collaborated with the United States Department of Transportation (USDOT) through the University Transportation Centers (UTC) program to research the comparative efficacy of statistical, machine learning, and deep learning approaches in weather-related crash risk prediction, establishing which methods perform optimally under differing conditions.

He is currently a Traffic/ITS Engineer at [Gresham Smith](https://www.greshamsmith.com/), supporting Georgia Department of Transportation projects across multiple districts, including traffic forecasting, safety analysis, and transportation systems planning. Abim is always enthusiastic about discussing advanced technologies, including AI, and their influence on the future of mobility.

Outside of work, Abim enjoys spending time with his family. He loves to watch movies and play games, and, fun fact, he's a big fan of baseball. See the [fun](/fun/) page for a glimpse of life outside the office.
